What the lights mean

Every state a rope can show, what it means and what to do about it. The strips are rendered by the same code that drives the wall; this page never changes the wall.

Strips read from the wire on the left to the far end on the right; on the wall the wire is at the bottom. Reversing a rope flips the active region only; the dark foot and the cap stay where they are.
