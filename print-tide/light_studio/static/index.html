Your wall, left to right →

Each card is one physical bay. Swap which printer drives a rope, or move a bay to match where it really hangs. Every rope shows 90 positions of printer state from the wire end, then a fixed 10-position cap at the far end that never shows state.

Available · resting, no motion Preparing · rising sweep Printing · water rising through darker bands, rain Paused · slow breath, steady end marks Error · deep fast breath Collect · lightest, until the door opens Stopped early · dark and still, until the door opens Offline · heartbeat Unknown · dashes Far-end cap · always on, never state

Progress is measured across the 90-position status region only — the cap is not part of the percentage. Reversing a rope changes the direction the status region fills; it never moves the cap, which stays at the end opposite the wire.
