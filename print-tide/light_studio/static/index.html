How a rope is laid out (100 positions)
bottom 30 · always dark 60 active · 0–100 % fills upward cap 10

Physical position 0 is the data-in wire at the bottom. The bottom 30 positions are inactive and always dark, the next 60 carry printer state (progress is measured across these 60 only), and the last 10 are an always-on cap that never shows state. Reversing a rope changes the fill direction inside positions 30–89 only; the dark foot and the cap never move.
